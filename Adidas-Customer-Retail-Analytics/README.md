# 👟 Adidas Customer Retail Analytics (Power BI Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Adidas customer retail data to understand **customer retention, churn behavior, loyalty impact, and sales performance** using **Power BI dashboards**.  
The goal is to provide **actionable business insights** that help improve customer engagement, reduce churn, and increase customer lifetime value (CLV).

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze customer churn and retention trends
- Identify high-value and repeat customers
- Evaluate the impact of loyalty programs and promotions
- Compare store and channel performance
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- **Power BI** – Data modeling & dashboard creation  
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning & preprocessing  
- **Data Analysis & Visualization**  
- **Customer Segmentation & KPI Analysis**  
- **Business Insight Generation**

---

## 📂 Datasets Used
All datasets are available inside the `datasets/` folder.

| Dataset Name | Description |
|--------------|------------|
| **Adidas_Customer_Transactional.xlsx** | Customer purchase transactions |
| **Customer_Demographics_Adidas.xlsx** | Customer age, gender, income level |
| **Customer_Churned.xlsx** | Churn status of customers |
| **Adidas_Store_Locations.xlsx** | Store type and regional details |
| **Loyalty_Metadata.xlsx** | Loyalty tier and membership information |

---

## 🔄 Data Preparation Steps
- Removed duplicate records
- Handled missing values
- Standardized categorical fields (Region, Store Type, Loyalty Tier)
- Created calculated fields for KPIs such as:
  - Churn Rate
  - Retention Rate
  - Repeat Rate
  - Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Online channels show higher churn compared to physical stores**
- **Loyalty program members have significantly lower churn rates**
- **High-value customers contribute a major share of total revenue**
- **Promotions increase purchase frequency, especially for mid-tier loyalty customers**
- **Certain regions and store types require targeted retention strategies**

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Strengthen loyalty programs with **personalized rewards and exclusive offers**
- Improve engagement for online customers to reduce churn
- Focus marketing efforts on **high-value and repeat customers**
- Use targeted promotions instead of blanket discounts
- Enhance in-store experience to leverage lower churn rates
